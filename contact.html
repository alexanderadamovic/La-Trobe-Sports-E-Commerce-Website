Contact & Location

Visit Us

The La Trobe Sports facilities are located on the main La Trobe University Melbourne Campus in Bundoora, Victoria. Centreway, La Trobe University, Bundoora VIC 3086.

Getting Here

La Trobe Sports can be easily accessible. You can drive to the facilities, catch the 301 from Reservoir station that takes you directly to the Bundoora campus, or if you're on campus you can walk over to the facilities.
